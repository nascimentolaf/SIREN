    Round-robin (rr)#

    Classic cyclic round-robin over flows: channel-independent, deadline-agnostic. It is the reference "no policy at all" baseline.

    Source: baseline_algorithms/rr/ (main.cpp, rr.cpp). Shared behaviour (configuration selection, job model, timing, output) is described in Baselines.

    Policy#

    The scheduler keeps one FIFO queue per flow and a round-robin pointer over the flows. At every scheduling instant, while a HARQ process is free:

    1. Starting at the pointer, take the first flow whose queue is non-empty and whose head job's B fits in the RBs still free. Flows whose head does not fit are skipped for this decision.
    2. Start that job on the lowest-index free HARQ process, at the current TTI boundary, for its full duration C.
    3. Move the pointer to the flow after the one just served.

    So each decision serves at most one segment of a flow before moving on; segments of the same packet, and several releases of the same flow, queue behind each other in FIFO order. When nothing can start, time jumps to the next release or completion.

    Specifics#
    config.objective round_robin
    Default output (no 3rd argument) ./<flowset stem>_rr.json
    Reads SINR_DB yes
    Extra environment none
    Extra report fields none

    A job runs to completion even after its deadline; the verdict comes from comparing each packet's last TB with its deadline (see Analysis).
