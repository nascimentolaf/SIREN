    QoS-PF (qospf)#

    QoS-aware proportional fairness, after M. Seliem, U. Roedig, C. Sreenan and D. Pesch, "QoS-Aware Proportional Fairness Scheduling for Multi-Flow 5G UEs: A Smart Factory Perspective", ACM MSWiM 2025, adapted to this framework's job model.

    Source: baseline_algorithms/qospf/ (main.cpp, qospf.cpp) and the 3GPP 5QI table in baseline_algorithms/common/qos_data.h. Shared behaviour is described in Baselines.

    Per-flow context#

    Computed once, before simulating:

    • Configuration: the shared selection rule (Baselines).
    • 5QI and priority: the flow's 5qi from the catalogue, or, if absent, one inferred by matching deadline and error against TS 23.501 Table 5.7.4-1. Its default priority level is normalised over the flowset to P_i in [0, 1], the most important (lowest level) flow getting 1. If every flow has the same level, all get 1.
    • GBR target: GBR_i = S_i × 8 / T_i bits/ms, the rate that delivers one packet per period.

    The log prints, per flow, the 5QI (declared or inferred), the priority level and the GBR.

    Policy#

    At every decision instant (a TTI with a release or a free HARQ process), as in the paper's per-TTI algorithm, every ready job is ranked once by

    M_i(t) = U_i(t) / R_i(t)
U_i(t) = ALPHA × D_i(t) + BETA × G_i(t) + GAMMA × P_i

D_i(t) = 1 / max(TTI, absolute deadline - t_up - t)   delay urgency
G_i(t) = max(0, 1 - R_i(t) / GBR_i)               GBR deficit
P_i                                               normalised 5QI priority
R_i(t)                                            the flow's average throughput, bits/ms

    and jobs are then started in that order, each on the lowest-index free process if its B fits in the free RBs (otherwise the next job is tried), until no process is free. The D term is floored at one TTI (the smallest actionable remaining time), which keeps it within about an order of magnitude of G and P (both in [0, 1]) so the weights mean what they say. The remaining time is counted to the deadline less the upper-layer delay t_up, the time the TB itself has: the packet is delivered only t_up after its last TB ends. Ties go to the earliest absolute deadline, then the application name.

    R_i is an exponential moving average with factor PF_EMA_LAMBDA, the weight of the newest sample, updated after each TTI as the authors' implementation updates it:

    • a flow with data queued in the TTI takes the sample bits_i / TTI, the payload of the jobs it started there (0 if none);
    • over the following TTIs, a flow still holding queued data takes zero samples, and a flow with nothing queued keeps its average.

    R_i starts at 1e-6, so a flow never served has a very large metric.

    Correspondence with the paper and its code#

    The paper (Sec. III-C) defines the metric and the per-TTI algorithm: compute M_i for every flow, sort, allocate in that order. It names an "exponential moving average of the throughput" for R_i but gives neither its update rule nor its weight. Its implementation (footnote 1: pull request 1 of MohamedSeliem/Simu5G, at the cited commit 6e0daf7f) is Simu5G's PF scheduler with its score multiplied by a fixed QoS weight: no ALPHA, BETA, GAMMA, no delay urgency or GBR deficit. That weight starts with the factor (10 − 5QI + 1), negative for every 5QI of the catalogue (82 to 88), so it cannot be followed as written. This scheduler therefore keeps the paper's metric and per-TTI ranking, and takes from the code what the paper leaves open: when and for which flows R_i is updated, and its weight (Simu5G's pfAlpha, 0.95, which the released configurations do not override).

    Paper / code Here
    M_i = U_i / R_i, U_i = α D_i + β G_i + γ P_i (Eqs. 1, 2) the same, with one α, β, γ for every flow, as in the paper's evaluation
    D_i: inverse of the remaining time to the deadline the same, floored at one TTI
    G_i: GBR deficit max(0, 1 − R_i / GBR_i), GBR_i the rate of one packet per period
    P_i: priority from the 5QI, normalised the 5QI's default priority level, normalised over the flowset
    each TTI: compute M_i, sort, allocate PRBs each decision instant: rank the ready jobs once, start them in that order
    R_i (code): after the TTI, bytes granted, flows with queued data only, weight pfAlpha = 0.95 the same, in bits per ms
    PRBs per TTI to a flow's buffer the framework's jobs: B RBs on one HARQ process for C

    Parameters#

    All four are required; the binary stops if one is missing.

    Variable parameters.sh Meaning
    ALPHA 0.4 weight of delay urgency
    BETA 0.3 weight of GBR deficit
    GAMMA 0.3 weight of 5QI priority
    PF_EMA_LAMBDA 0.95 weight of the newest sample in the throughput average, in (0, 1] (the authors' Simu5G default)

    0.4 / 0.3 / 0.3 is the paper's "Balanced (Default)" setting; it also publishes Delay-Tuned 0.7 / 0.2 / 0.1 and Fairness-Tuned 0.2 / 0.2 / 0.6:

    ALPHA=0.7 BETA=0.2 GAMMA=0.1 ./scripts/run_paper_experiment.sh
