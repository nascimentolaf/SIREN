    Proportional fair (pf)#

    Classic proportional fairness: serve the job whose achievable rate is largest relative to its flow's average past rate. No deadline and no RB-cost term enter the metric.

    Source: baseline_algorithms/pf/ (main.cpp, pf.cpp). Shared behaviour is described in Baselines.

    Policy#

    At every scheduling instant (a TTI with a release or a free HARQ process), while a process is free, each flow offers its next ready job if the job's B fits in the free RBs; the offers are ranked by

    metric_i = r_i / R_i
r_i      = useful payload bytes of the job / its duration C   (achievable rate, bytes/ms)
R_i      = the flow's average past rate (bytes/ms), as at the start of the TTI

    and the highest metric starts on the lowest-index free process. The offers are then taken afresh, so a flow just served offers its next job in the same TTI: the scheduler is work-conserving, like the other baselines. Ties go to flowset order, then release order, then segment order. C is the worst case, A attempts: under EXEC_TIME_MODE=sampled the process is held for the attempts the job really takes, but ranking on those would assume the scheduler knows them in advance.

    R_i is a single exponential moving average over TTIs, as in the standard definition R_i(t) = (1 - 1/t_c) R_i(t-1) + (1/t_c) r_i(t):

    • after each TTI the flow takes the sample r_i(t), the summed rate of the jobs it started in the TTI (0 if none), with weight PF_ALPHA (= 1/t_c);
    • every TTI the flow is not served it takes a zero sample, so its average decays by (1 - PF_ALPHA) per TTI, compounded over the gap between two scheduling events. That decay is what lets an unserved flow climb the ranking.

    All flows start at R_i = PF_INITIAL_THROUGHPUT.

    Parameters#

    Variable parameters.sh Binary default Meaning
    PF_ALPHA 0.01 0.1 1/t_c: weight of the newest sample. Memory = 1/PF_ALPHA TTIs; 100 TTIs = 25 ms at the default
    PF_INITIAL_THROUGHPUT 1.0 1.0 initial R_i, bytes/ms; must be > 0

    Specifics#
    config.objective proportional_fair
    Default output (no 3rd argument) ./<flowset stem>_pf.json
    Reads SINR_DB yes
    Extra report fields none

    PF is the only baseline whose ordering depends on achieved service. Under EXEC_TIME_MODE=sampled an early completion changes the averages and so every later decision, which is why PF is the method most affected by early HARQ termination (see Baselines).
