    About us#

    This framework is the experimental side of the paper SIREN: Table-Driven Real-Time MAC Scheduler. The paper proposes SIREN, the table-driven scheduler that the code calls ilp. Its four authors are researchers at the Laboratoire de Traitement et Communication de l'Information (LTCI), Télécom Paris.
